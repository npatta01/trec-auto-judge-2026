The sanitized [RAG receipt](../output/submissions/ntp-rag-pairwise-10-v1/receipt.json) and [RAGTIME receipt](../output/submissions/ntp-ragtime-pairwise-10-v1/receipt.json) preserve IDs and provenance. Four sanitized evidence/combined confirmations are summarized in the [ledger](submission-ledger.md); original safe status files are inside the encrypted private archive.

Original source bundles, private inputs, cached completions, stages, references/checklists, detailed historical docs, accounting, frozen image and encrypted claim receipts are in the [encrypted recovery release](recovery.md). They are not ordinary public assets or prerequisites for reading these numeric packages. Do not upload the private archive as a TIRA data package.
