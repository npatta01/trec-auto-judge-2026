Three judging approaches

Pairwise

Compare pairs of answers in both orders. Fit a Bradley–Terry model to estimate each answer’s win chances.

Evidence

Build one checklist per question. Grade what each answer covers and check how well its citations support it.

Combined

Give the Pairwise and Evidence scores equal weight, then use the blended scores to rank systems.
