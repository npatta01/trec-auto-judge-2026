How should we judge cited answers?

We tested three ways to score answers and rank the systems that wrote them. We used GPT-6 Luna for all six submissions across TREC’s RAG and RAGTIME tracks.

Slides Read the paper Architecture walkthrough

Architecture

We score each answer two ways. Pairwise judges which answer is better. Evidence grades what each answer covers and what its citations support.

Read the video walkthrough
  1. Given several cited answers to a question, Pairwise compares answers in both orders. In the example, one win and one tie give Answer A 0.75 credit against B. Bradley–Terry fits ratings from the judged pairs.
  2. Evidence checks two required items, hours and wellbeing. Answer A covers both. One citation fully supports the hours, and the other supports reduced stress but leaves the profit claim unverified. Their support credits average to 0.75.
  3. Combined averages the Pairwise and Evidence scores for each answer. Averaging across questions gives each system a score. The organizers compare the resulting ranking with the official ranking.

Pairwise

Compare up to ten opponents per answer and judge both presentation orders. When we judge a subset of the pairs, fit a Bradley–Terry model and average each answer’s estimated win chances against every opponent.

Evidence

Pool excerpts from the systems’ citations to generate a reference answer and one checklist per question. Grade checklist coverage and check each answer’s own sources in separate passes.

Combined

Average the two scores for each answer, with equal weight. Then average scores across questions to rank systems.

How the Evidence score differs by track
RAG
Average request coverage, citation precision, and citation recall.
RAGTIME
Average grounded factual coverage and partial-credit support from each segment’s first citation.

RAG

Request coverage alone agreed much more closely with human preferences than the Evidence composite. Citation precision and recall contribute two-thirds of the composite and each had negative rank agreement on its own.

RAGTIME

Evidence had the highest agreement of our three approaches with the official F1 ranking, which uses mostly human assessments. Our best individual measure scores each answer by the fraction of its segments fully supported by their first citation. See how sentence support is counted.
